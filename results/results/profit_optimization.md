# Profit Optimization & Customer Targeting Strategy

## Business Objective
Maximize projected net profit from a customer acquisition campaign while operating under a fixed marketing budget.

## Campaign Assumptions
- Marketing budget: $15,000
- Cost per customer contacted: $1.50
- Maximum number of contacts: 10,000

## Strategy Comparison

| Strategy | Projected Net Profit |
|---|---:|
| Segment-based targeting | $127,275.50 |
| Individual-level predictive targeting | $140,398.32 |
| Incremental projected profit | $13,122.82 |

## Financial Impact
The individual-level targeting strategy projected approximately 10.31% higher net profit than the segment-based benchmark.

## Methodology
1. Estimate customer response probabilities and predicted funding amounts.
2. Calculate expected financial value after marketing costs.
3. Rank prospective customers by estimated value.
4. Select the top 10,000 customers under the campaign budget.
5. Compare projected financial outcomes with segment-based targeting.

## Recommendation
Prioritize individual-level, expected-value-based targeting under the modeled assumptions, while validating model performance and financial assumptions before operational implementation.

## Limitations
All profit figures are model-based projections rather than realized financial returns. The analysis does not establish causal lift or guarantee future campaign performance.
